## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-22

### Added

- Initial release of Modern Frontend Engineering.
- Agent-independent instructions for compatible AI coding agents.

- Repository inspection and context-aware implementation workflows.
- Engineering principles focused on correctness, maintainability, simplicity, and production quality.
- Cross-cutting anti-AI-slop standards to prevent generic, superficial, incomplete, and unverified output.
